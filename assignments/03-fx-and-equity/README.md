# FX and equity | Carry trades and valuation

The assignment connects exchange-rate frameworks to the yen carry-trade unwind and compares valuation approaches for S&P Global and Palo Alto Networks.

## FX frameworks

Use purchasing power parity for relative price levels, uncovered interest parity to discuss expected returns, and covered interest parity to price a hedge from observable funding rates.

## Hedge choice

Compare forwards, futures, and non-deliverable forwards by settlement cash flows, margin needs, and market access.

## Equity valuation

Compare dividend discount and firm cash flow methods with relative benchmarks. Growth, reinvestment, and terminal assumptions have different consequences for a mature business and a growth business.
