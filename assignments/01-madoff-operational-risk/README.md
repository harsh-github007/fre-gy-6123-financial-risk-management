# Madoff | Operational risk and due diligence

The case reconstructs the claimed split-strike conversion strategy and traces how the investment-advisory fraud reached clients through feeder funds and intermediaries.

## Strategy plausibility

Compare the claimed equity collar with the scale, liquidity, and settlement records needed to execute it. An explainable payoff establishes what trades should exist and what independent records should show.

## Risk transmission

Separately track the people reporting performance, the parties holding client money, and the intermediaries distributing the product. Exposure can concentrate through multiple routes that appear independent in a portfolio report.

## Controls

Prioritize independent custody and trade confirmation, auditor due diligence, and reconciliation between broker statements and reported positions. Review unusually smooth returns against the market behavior of the stated strategy.
