# Rates and fixed income | Sensitivity and forward pricing

The assignment derives bond price sensitivity from discounted cash flows and examines the short-term rates used in USD funding and valuation.

## Duration and convexity

The first derivative of price gives local yield sensitivity, and the second derivative captures curvature. Compare the duration-only and duration-plus-convexity approximations with full repricing as the yield move grows.

## Benchmarks

Compare the economic exposures behind short-term rates, including the transition from LIBOR to SOFR. Tenor, collateral, and credit exposure all matter when choosing a benchmark.

## Forward rates

Use the ratio of discount factors across maturities to solve for the future-period rate implied by the curve. Read the result alongside the compounding convention and interval used in the calculation.
